吃的不只是晚餐，是一整晚的熱鬧

什麼是夜市？What is a night market?

夜市是傍晚後出現的露天或街區型市集，由小吃攤、遊戲攤、服飾、生活用品與附近店家組成。它既是吃晚餐的地方，也是台灣人下班、放學後散步聊天的生活空間。

A Taiwanese night market is an evening street market filled with food stalls, games, clothing, everyday goods and permanent shops. It is more than dinner—it is a social space where people stroll, snack and unwind.

FIRST-TIMER GUIDE

夜市怎麼逛？

  1. 先走一圈：看看排隊人潮與價目表，再決定想吃什麼。
  2. 帶少量現金：許多攤販仍以現金為主。
  3. 邊走邊吃要留意：部分攤位有座位，垃圾請交回攤商或丟到指定垃圾桶。
  4. 勇敢開口：可以說「一份，謝謝」、「不要辣」或「可以少冰嗎？」

Walk around first, keep small cash, return rubbish to the stall when appropriate, and use simple phrases such as “One portion, please” or “Not spicy.”

🥟

可以期待什麼？What to expect

常見蚵仔煎、滷味、鹽酥雞、地瓜球、胡椒餅與各式飲料；每座夜市都有自己的招牌。

Look for oyster omelets, braised snacks, fried chicken, sweet potato balls, pepper buns and drinks. Each market has its own specialties.

🌙

In Taipei, start with Ningxia, Raohe Street or Shilin. Opening days and hours may change, so check before visiting.

理解一座城市的信仰與人情

台灣廟宇Taiwanese Temples

台灣廟宇是宗教場所，也是社區文化中心。你可以欣賞建築、觀察祭祀文化；入內請放低音量，不踩門檻，拍照前留意現場標示。

Temples are both religious spaces and community centers. Visitors are welcome to appreciate the architecture and observe local traditions. Speak softly, step over—not on—the threshold, and check photography signs.

關

忠義與安定 · ZHONGSHAN

行天宮 Xingtian Temple

主祀關聖帝君，是台北重要的民間信仰中心。廟方倡導簡樸祭祀，不設香爐與供桌；參拜時合掌致意即可。

Dedicated primarily to Guan Sheng Di Jun, Xingtian Temple is a major center of folk belief. It promotes simple worship without incense burners or offering tables; visitors may show respect by placing their palms together.

  • 捷運行天宮站附近
  • 適合觀察：收驚文化、志工服務、現代都市信仰

姻緣與老城 · DADAOCHENG

霞海城隍廟 Taipei Xia-Hai City God Temple

位於迪化街，以月下老人信仰聞名，也供奉城隍爺與眾多神祇。廟內空間不大、香火鼎盛，可依現場人員指引參拜。

Located on Dihua Street, this compact and lively temple is famous for the Matchmaker God, while also honoring the City God and other deities. Follow on-site guidance when worshipping.

  • 可與迪化街、大稻埕安排同日散步
  • 參拜月老重點是誠實介紹自己與理想對象
參拜小提醒 Temple etiquette服裝整潔、保持安靜、不擋住參拜動線；不確定時，禮貌詢問廟方志工即可。Dress respectfully, keep pathways clear, and ask temple volunteers if you are unsure.

購物商圈的組成How shopping districts work

台灣的「商圈」不是只有一座百貨公司，而是由街道、商場、捷運站、餐飲、生活服務、文化空間與住宅共同形成的步行生活圈。不同巷弄常有不同氣氛，適合慢慢探索。

A Taiwanese shopping district is not a single mall. It is a walkable network of streets, stores, transit, food, services, cultural spaces and homes—often with a different mood on every lane.

台北地下街Taipei City Mall

串聯台北車站周邊交通節點的地下商場，可分成服飾雜貨、電子商品、動漫模型、餐飲與服務區域。雨天、炎熱天氣都很適合逛。

An underground mall connecting transport hubs around Taipei Main Station, with fashion, electronics, anime goods, food and services. It is a reliable rainy-day or hot-weather option.

中山商圈Zhongshan Shopping District

以捷運中山站、線形公園與赤峰街為核心，聚集百貨、選物店、咖啡店、藝文空間、老屋小店與餐廳，呈現新舊混合的城市風格。

Centered around Zhongshan MRT Station, the linear park and Chifeng Street, this area blends department stores, boutiques, cafés, cultural venues, renovated old houses and restaurants.
